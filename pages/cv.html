About

My interests span Theoretical Computer Science and Machine Learning.

Currently, I am working on machine learning—ensemble models, gating networks, Positive-Unlabeled (PU) learning, and heavily imbalanced learning. And, computational linguistics—Filipino, phonology, and orthography. I belong to the MangoCats, an undergraduate research group being advised by Nathaniel Oco whose focus is on word confusability and problems related to it.

My previous work includes morpholigically rich languages their computational complexity, programming languages, and some work on computer graphics.

A Rust library for phonetic similarity. This project is part of our undergraduate thesis in the MangoCats group.

  • Designing and building a Rust library implementing string similarity algorithms for phonetic and orthographic string comparison.
